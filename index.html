Causal inference · Healthcare financeFeatured

Insurance Charges & the Causal Effect of Smoking

How much does smoking actually cost in health insurance, after controlling for age, BMI and everything else?

+$23,850per year, causal effect
≈ $8.3Kprediction test RMSE
~92%of effect variation driven by BMI

Built a prediction pipeline on 1,338 policyholders, then estimated the smoking effect three independent ways. All three landed within $25 of each other, and smokers with BMI above 30 face by far the highest costs.

scikit-learnCausalMLDoWhyXGBoost
Read the case study

Approach

  • Stratified train/test split on smoking status (only ~20% of the sample smokes).
  • Outlier review with IQR and Isolation Forest; high BMIs kept as medically plausible.
  • Pipeline + ColumnTransformer; linear regression on log(charges), validated with 10-fold CV (≈ $8,590 ± $850, so no overfitting).
  • Causal: S-Learner and T-Learner (XGBoost) in CausalML, plus a DoWhy backdoor model.

Robustness

  • Adding a random common cause left the estimate unchanged.
  • A placebo treatment dropped the effect to about −$105, as it should.

Limitations I flagged

  • Observational data; diet and exercise are unmeasured. BMI is partly affected by smoking, so it's an imperfect control.

Halloween & Crime in Montréal

Can we predict high-crime days, and does Halloween actually change crime?

363K+police reports, 2015–2025
0.81cross-validated AUC
+22%mischief during Halloween

Halloween changes the mix of crime, not the total: mischief and robbery rise about 22–24% while overall daily crime doesn't. The ensemble beat a seasonal baseline on both held-out years.

scikit-learnPyMCRandom forestNeural nets
Read the case study

Approach

  • Cleaned and translated French categories, aggregated to daily counts, engineered calendar, holiday, COVID and lag features.
  • Leak-free sliding-window time-series CV (5 folds, 29-day gap); 2024–25 held out until the end.
  • Compared logistic regression, Bayesian negative binomial (PyMC), random forest and a neural net; rank-averaged an ensemble of the three with the most different errors.

Results

  • Held-out AUC 0.66 vs. 0.65 (2024) and 0.67 vs. 0.65 (2025) against the seasonal-naive baseline.
  • No reliable district differences (likelihood-ratio p = 0.067), so the effect is treated as citywide.

Does More AI Mean More Output?

Do people who use AI tools more actually get more done, and what makes them satisfied?

8.6 hdaily use where gains plateau
0.63test R², productivity
9models benchmarked

More AI use raises productivity with sharply diminishing returns, and perceived accuracy drives satisfaction. The interpretable quadratic regression beat random forest, boosting, SVR, KNN and a neural net.

statsmodelsRobust OLSscikit-learnExcel
Read the case study

Hypotheses

  • ✓ More usage → higher productivity (diminishing returns)
  • ✓ Higher perceived accuracy → higher satisfaction (+0.86 per point)
  • ✗ Paid vs. free tools · ✗ Students vs. professionals

Why data quality mattered

  • One respondent reporting 23 hours of AI use a day pushed cross-validated R² below zero. Treating implausible values as missing raised it to 0.53.

Deliverable

  • A results workbook with every table plus an interactive calculator: enter hours of use, get predicted productivity and the marginal gain of one more hour.

Deep learning · Computer visionMGSC 673

CNN vs. Transfer Learning on CIFAR-10

Train a small model from scratch, or fine-tune one pre-trained on ImageNet?

94.8%fine-tuned ResNet-18
91.6%CNN built from scratch
9×fewer parameters in the CNN

Transfer learning won on accuracy in 5 epochs instead of 30. The scratch CNN came within 3.3 points at a fraction of the size, making it the better choice for phones and embedded devices.

PyTorchtorchvisionCNNsMixed precision
Read the case study

Approach

  • 45K / 5K / 10K train-validation-test split; random-crop and flip augmentation; test set used once.
  • CNN: three conv blocks (64→128→256) with batch norm and dropout, SGD + OneCycle schedule.
  • ResNet-18: 2 epochs training only the new head, then 3 epochs fine-tuning everything with AdamW.

Insight

  • Frozen ImageNet features alone reached only 85.3%; unfreezing all layers added 9.6 points. Cats (80.6%) and dogs (86.6%) were the hardest classes.
